## 0. What Raees's job actually is (Afnan, 23 Sept 2026, verbatim in spirit)

Raees manages the store **and** pays its vendors — on credit or cash terms.
Some payments are monthly bills (water, internet); maintenance cash goes
through him; his rider **Noman** does the buying. Every purchase must carry a
**rate** (thread came in → which vendor → at what rate → what is that
vendor's balance and terms). Bottled water and gas are **metered daily**
(40 bottles today, 20 tomorrow; kg delivered minus kg left in the returned
cylinder) at a fixed rate, and the month's bill is generated by us and
checked against the vendor's. The store must be **100% accurate**; purchases
land in store inventory. Vendor profiles are created through a **series of
questions** matched to their terms, and export to **Excel**. Cash in is
**Cash or MCB transfer only**. A cash statement exports to Excel **by date
range**. The entry log must be **clean, uniform, accounts-friendly,
record-keeping friendly**.

So this is **purchasing + accounts payable + metered consumables**, with a
petty-cash drawer inside it — not a petty-cash app. The old `js/store-cash.js`
was the latter, and its ten defects are listed in the session that replaced
it (summarised in CLAUDE.md under "Store Accounts"). It is **deleted**, not
patched: its model (four equal wallets, mutable stored balances, spend that
vanished from reports, categories welded to vendors) could not be bent into
this.

## 1. Decisions taken (Claude's calls — overrule here, not in code review)

| Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Order first or receipt first? | **Receipt first.** A purchase is recorded when goods/bill arrive. No PO-to-vendor state machine in wave 1. | Every purchase Afnan described is "it came in, at this rate". An order pipeline is a second module; add it when a vendor short-delivers often enough to need it. |
| Rate storage | **On the purchase line, never on the item.** The vendor's *rate card* is DERIVED from their purchase lines (last / min / max / count per item). | One source of truth; a renegotiated rate is simply the next purchase. Nothing to keep in step. |
| Non-stock purchases | **Same entry.** A purchase line either names a store item (posts to inventory) or is free text with a category. | One row shape is the "uniform log" requirement. |
| Money accounts | **Two: `cash` (drawer) and `mcb`.** SadaPay/EasyPaisa dropped. | Afnan: "other bank options are irrelevant". |
| Balances | **Always derived from the ledger; never stored on an account doc.** A month close stores a checkpoint. | The old module's stored balance was rewritten on every page open from a 5,000-row cap. |
| Corrections | **Void, never edit.** A voided row stays visible (struck through) with who/when/why. | Record-keeping. An edited ledger is not a ledger. |
| Dates | **Settable on every entry, default today (local time).** | The old module stamped UTC-today on everything; before 5am PKT that is yesterday. |
| Vendor terms | `cash` (pay on delivery) · `credit` (N days, optional limit) · `monthly` (bill day, expected amount) · `weekly` (bill weekdays — several, default the payable days; expected amount; added 23 Sept at Afnan's ask). **Payable days** are a setting, default Wednesday and Saturday; the ledger says what is owed on a pay day. — plus a **meter** for consumables (`count` or `weighed`, unit, rate). | Matches the four shapes Afnan named: trims on credit, walk-in cash, utilities, water/gas — and a weekly bill. |
| Purchase kind | `Stock purchase` (lines: item code · qty · unit · rate; a store item posts to inventory) or `Expense / service` (what was done + amount; nothing touches inventory). Default follows the vendor's kind. Both are `type:'purchase'`; an expense is one description line at qty 1 with `expense:true`. (Added 23 Sept 2026 — Afnan: a paint job "has nothing to do with inventory".) | One uniform ledger, and a service bill no longer has to be faked as a stock line. |
| Consumable log PDF | `Print log (PDF)` on the consumables page and on the bill's entry detail (vendor page): one row per day of the month (dashes for an unlogged day), a TOTAL row, and a Billing section (month total · bill generated · vendor's figure · variance). Through the print engine's `consumable-log` variant, `urduLevel` minimal. (Added 23 Sept 2026 — Afnan: "what happened on each day + total billing".) | The bill is checkable against the log on paper, and the vendor can be handed the same sheet. |
| Paying from OTHER | A payment's **Paid from** offers Cash, MCB or **Other** — settled outside both books (a director's own pocket, a set-off, someone else covering it). The vendor's payable drops; neither Cash nor MCB moves; the note is required and is the only record of how. Not a money account: no balance, no book, no tile. (24 Sept 2026 — Afnan: "OTHER should be here as well but there is no credit debit account of other it is settled without a record".) | A settlement the books never saw still has to clear the vendor's account, and inventing a third wallet for it would be a balance nobody could count. |
| Admin correction tools | Afnan only, by username (`_acctIsSuper()` / rules `isAcctSuper()`): **Edit (admin)** and **Delete (admin)** on any entry's detail; **Reopen** the last closed month and **Reset Store Accounts** (typed RESET; entries, meter logs, closes; vendors optional) on the review page; **Delete vendor…** on a vendor's page, refused while any entry (live or in a closed month) names the vendor, taking a consumable vendor's meter logs with it. Inventory is never touched by any of them. (Added 23 Sept 2026 — Afnan: "a button in afnan view only to reset + edit + delete record of things".) | Void-never-edit stays the rule for the workflow; one person holds the repair tools, and the rules hold the same line. |
| Overdue | FIFO: payments settle the oldest credit purchases first; an unpaid purchase older than `creditDays` is overdue. | Standard aging, no allocation UI needed. |
| Runner floats | **Real, kept.** `float_out` to a named person; purchases can be paid **from a float**; `float_in` returns change. Outstanding is derived. Age warnings at 3 / 7 days (settings). | Noman is the buyer. The old itemized "issue" had no accountability step. |
| Approval | **Warn, never block.** Owner-set `approvalLimit` and `receiptRequiredAbove` flag an entry `needsReview`; owners clear it from a Review queue. | This codebase's standing rule (recipe gate, pattern warning): a hard block stops production at 2am for paperwork. |
| Cash in confirmation | An owner recording "sent ₨X" creates a **pending** entry Raees confirms; Raees recording it himself posts at once. Pending never counts toward balance. | Two sides, one record. |
| Month close | `acct_closes/{YYYY-MM}`: book cash vs counted cash, variance, note, payables snapshot. **A closed month accepts no new entries and no voids.** Loading reads entries since the last close plus the checkpoint. | Bounds reads (the Store's 429 lesson) and makes the books final. |
| Consumables | Daily log per vendor (`acct_meter_logs`), month total × rate → **Generate bill** creates the credit purchase once (keyed `vendorId_month`); the vendor's own bill amount is recorded beside it and the variance shown. | Afnan's water/gas requirement exactly. |
| Vendor creation | A **5-step wizard**: who → what they supply → how you pay (branches by terms; meter setup for consumables) → contact → opening balance → summary. | "A series of questions to customise the profile according to the term". |
| Excel | Cash statement (Cash book · MCB book · All · Summary) **by date range**; vendor statement by date range; payables as of today. Vendored SheetJS, no CDN. | Asked for by name. |
| Categories, runners, limits | Live in `acct_settings/main`, owner-edited, with defaults in code. **Categories only: Raees can add one from the purchase form** ("+ New category…"), written with an `updateMask` of just that field; the rule holds him to it. The picker is settings ∪ categories already on purchases. | Not a collection each. Afnan, 23 Sept: "option to create new category". |
| Existing data | **Nothing migrates automatically.** An owner-only *Import legacy cash ledger* maps old `store_cash_ledger` rows into the new ledger, idempotently (`legacyId`). | Unknown whether the old data is real; an import button is safe either way. |
| Audience | View: owners, managers, `store` role. Entry: owners + `store` (Raees). Admin (close, settings, void after review, import): owners. Mirrored in `firestore.rules` as `isStoreAccounts()`. | The one per-role grant the plan needed. |
| Not built (named so nobody files them as missed) | Purchase orders to vendors; cheques; a Noman login; PDF statements (Excel first); per-invoice payment allocation UI; multi-currency. | Each is a phase, not a variant. |

## 2. Data model

All reads/writes go through `js/store.js`'s REST helpers (`fsList`, `fsSet`,
`fsAdd`, `_fsRunQuery`) like the rest of the Store.

- **`acct_vendors/{id}`** — `name, kind (goods|service|utility|consumable),
  terms{mode, creditDays, creditLimit, billDay, expectedAmount},
  meter{type(count|weighed), unit, rate, label}, contact{person,phone,address},
  supplies[], notes, openingBalance, openingDate, active, createdAt/By,
  updatedAt`.
- **`acct_entries/{id}`** — THE ledger, one shape:
  `type (purchase|payment|cash_in|transfer|float_out|float_in|adjust|opening),
  date, month, ts, by, byName, vendorId, vendorName, person, account
  (cash|mcb|null), toAccount, source (cash|mcb|credit|float), floatId, amount,
  lines[{itemCode?, desc, qty, unit, rate, total, sizes?}], category, ref,
  note, photo, status (posted|pending|void), voidedAt/By/Reason,
  needsReview, reviewFlags[], reviewedAt/By, confirmedAt/By, stockPosted,
  stockError, meterKey, vendorBillAmount, legacyId`.
  Effects are derived by `_acctEffect(e)` — one function the ledger, the
  books, the vendor statement and the exports all read.
- **`acct_meter_logs/{vendorId}_{date}`** — `vendorId, date, month, qty,
  residual, net, rate, by, ts, note`.
- **`acct_settings/main`** — limits, categories, runners.
- **`acct_closes/{YYYY-MM}`** — checkpoint + sign-off.

## 3. Pages (`acct-*`, one `renderPage` line)

`acct-ledger` (books, KPIs, alerts, filters, entry) · `acct-vendors` /
