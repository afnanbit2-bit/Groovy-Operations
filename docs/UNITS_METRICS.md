# How units should be measured (Inventory Intel ▸ Article Explorer)

Raw units per week cannot compare a product live since 2025 with one live since Apr 2026, and cannot tell "selling well"
from "had more stock" or "was out of stock". Definitions below are from general merchandising practice (vendor
sources, unverified; no external figure is used as a threshold). Code: `_siAxStats`, `_siAxExposure`, `_siAxClassify` in `js/shopify.js`.

## 1. What the app holds (verified: code read and live read-only checks, 30 Sept 2026)

| Source | Holds | Does NOT hold |
|---|---|---|
| `shopify_line_items` (`shopify-order-sync.js:153-176`) | sku, title, size, `quantity`, unit `price` (before discounts), `order_created_at`, `financial_status` at sync time | discount amounts, per-line refunds, cancellations |
| `shopify_products` (`shopify-catalog-sync.js:170-190`) | per variant sku, price, status, `created_at`, `published_at` | inventory. **`published_at` is missing on all 2,126 live docs** (live check), `created_at` is Shopify creation (2021+), not a launch date |
| `shopify_inventory_snapshots` (`shopify-inventory-snapshot.js:128-153`, schedule `0 5,17 * * *`) | one doc per PKT day (the 22:00 run overwrites the 10:00 one): `items[inventoryItemId]={available,variant_id,sku}`, one location | receipts. Live: **128 docs, 2026-05-26..2026-09-30, no gaps**, 1,713 to 2,056 items each (QA read, 30 Sept). Stock history starts two months after sales history |
| `shopify_weekly_closes` | top SKU / category totals | anything per article |

Live-data findings handled in code: financial_status `voided` (3,261 rows) is excluded with `refunded`; negative `available`
(11,031 entries) is clamped to 0; items with no SKU are skipped; duplicate SKUs are summed per article; a snapshot with under 80% of
the median item count is dropped as truncated (none seen: counts only ever rose except 2026-09-25, 2018 to 2014).

**Cost of the history:** `_siAxEnsureHistory` does ONE `getDocs(query(orderBy('date','desc'), limit(150)))` when the Explorer opens, cached for
the session, never rejects (named error state + Retry). Today that is 128 document reads per session (each ~2k items; payload size is a hypothesis, not measured).
Non-merchandise (every priced sale under Rs 1, e.g. TIPQUIK-TG Tip/Gratuity: 11,000 units at Rs 0.01) is left out of the Explorer and reported in the Data quality expander.

**Voided and refunded units** (per article, counted window): `voided` = units on voided orders, `refunded` = units on orders refunded when synced, `void rate` = voided ÷ (net + voided + refunded). Use it for: a high void rate points at a payment, fraud or cancellation problem to investigate. They never enter net units, pace, sell-through or cover. Limit: financial_status is read at sync time, so later voids/refunds are not seen and both understate.

Other Inventory Intel tables still exclude `refunded` only, so their totals differ from the Explorer by the voided orders.

## 2. The ten metrics (each: formula, use, how to read, limit)

Counted window = from max(publish date, first synced order) to today. With no publish date (today: all products) an article that sold within 7 days of the
first synced order is treated as live at the data start, otherwise as launched on its first sale (labelled "first sale"). Missing inputs show "—", never 0.
Stock figures use only snapshot days; a day is **measurable** when snapshots of D-1 and D both exist, **in stock** if stock > 0 at the end of D-1 or D,
a **stock-out day** if both were 0.

| # | Metric | Formula | Use it for | Low / high | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Net units | non-refunded, non-voided units in the counted window | volume | low can just be few live days | later refunds/cancels unseen |
| 2 | Units per live week | units ÷ counted days × 7 | compare ages; reorder or stop | low slow or under-exposed | needs 7+ counted days |
| 3 | Sell-through % | sold ÷ (sold + stock left at the end of the span), over the longest contiguous measured span | reorder vs markdown | low: not moving; high: short of stock | needs 7+ contiguous snapshot days; span printed; fallback is the two loaded snapshots (labelled); never above 100%; stock lost without a sale counts as sold, restocked returns count as unsold |
| 4 | In-stock rate | in-stock days ÷ measurable days | separate "not selling" from "not available" | low: sales capped by supply | needs 7+ measurable days |
| 5 | Units per in-stock day | units on in-stock days ÷ in-stock days | true demand; size a reorder | well above per live day: often out | needs 7+ in-stock days |
| 6 | Stock-out days | measurable days with no stock | restock review; lost-sales risk | high: availability limits sales | counted, never extrapolated |
| 7 | Weeks of cover | on hand ÷ weekly pace (recency-weighted in-stock pace over up to 12 weeks, half-life 4 weeks; else 28-day pace); shown as a range (95% Poisson on the units behind it), top end capped: "more than 26 weeks" | when to reorder, overstock | < ~2 reorder, > ~26 overstock | assumes pace continues; none specific: merchandise units per month are flat (the August spike in raw data was the sub-Rs-1 tip SKU, now left out: 11,166 of August's 19,119 non-refunded units) |
| 8 | Momentum | last 28 d units against the 28 before; Rising/Fading only with 20+ units across both and a gap bigger than chance (z = (\|last − prev\| − 1) / √N > 1.96), Steady when 20+ and within chance, else "too few sales to tell" | rising or fading | never a bare percentage | needs 56 counted days |
| 9 | Share of category | the article's units in the last 28 days ÷ all units of the same product_type in the last 28 days | range planning | new articles start low | mixes ages |
| 10 | Age-normalised curve | cumulative units by weeks since launch (Compare ▸ Since launch) + first-28-days units | compare launches | steeper early = stronger | launch proxy is first sale until `published_at` exists |

**Received stock is inferred**, not recorded, and is a SUPPORTING figure only (it is not in sell-through): per day max(0, stock change + units sold), counted only when ≥ max(5, 10% of opening). Live data: most residuals are ±1–2 units (noise).

**Why sell-through is sold ÷ (sold + stock left), not sold ÷ (opening + received)** (30 Sept 2026, real dump: 128 daily snapshots, 339 articles with a span). The older formula exceeded 100% for **65 of 339** articles (37 of them classed Winner), e.g. GST001 Baggy Trousers | Black 105.1%: sold 1,025 of opening 150 + 825 inferred received. Cause, by plain-node accounting independent of the page: stock identity closing = opening + all increases − losses − sold holds for every article, and GST001's increases total **975**, not 825: the 150 units of increases smaller than the noise floor (max(5, 10% of opening) = 15 a day; e.g. +1…+4 almost every day, persistent, so not timing) were dropped from supply while the sales they belong to stayed in `sold`. Ruled out with numbers: sales on the first snapshot day (excluded by design: the span starts the day after the first snapshot), SKU spelling (no sold SKU is absent from the snapshots, 5 articles checked), negative-stock clamping (−14 units over 127 days for GST001), snapshot timing (shifting sales ±1 day makes the residuals worse, not better). The small increases are most likely restocked returns/cancellations (unverified: no return log exists). Counting every increase gives a floor (GST001 91.1%) and cannot exceed 100%; sold ÷ (sold + closing stock) is the same identity with the net of increases and losses and needs no inference: GST001 97.3%, median over all articles 0.50 (was 0.53), 0 above 100%, and only 14 of 339 articles change band (under 5% / 5–20% / 20–60% / 60%+). It is also the standard retail definition. Class thresholds are unchanged.
Supporting figures: last-28-day pace, sizes in stock, lost-sales risk (0-stock size that sold in 28 d; a flag, not a quantity), selling weeks, peak week, average price (before discounts).

**Definition choices vs. an independent oracle** (`scratchpad/oracle`, 5 hand-computed fixtures; every difference is a deliberate definition, none a bug): counted window starts at the first synced
order (oracle: live date); in-stock day = stock > 0 at either end of the day (oracle: end of day); 7-day minimum for rates and stock figures (oracle computes from 4–6 days); momentum uses rolling 28-day windows and needs 56 counted days
(oracle: complete Monday weeks); weeks of cover uses the in-stock pace; receipt noise floor. Net units, category share, age curve, weekly counts agree exactly.

## 3. Scorecard — classes v2 (30 Sept 2026; defaults, not facts)
Demand D = units per in-stock day (exposure-adjusted; falls back to the plain rate ÷ 7 and says "partly unverified" when stock history is too short). pD = mid-rank percentile of D, (below + 0.5 × equal) ÷ n, among classed articles of **similar age** (bands by counted days 28–55, 56–111, 112+; a band under 30 articles merges into the next one up). A pool still under 30 uses fixed bands: Winner 2.7, Solid 0.5, Steady 0.2 units per in-stock day. First match wins:
1. **Too early**: < 28 counted days (no class). **Not rated**: no stock data, or no demand figure.
2. **Dead stock**: no sale in the last 28 counted days with stock on hand.
3. **Stock-constrained**: in stock on < 70% of measured days AND demand above the median (pD ≥ 0.50).
4. **Winner**: pD ≥ 0.90 AND in stock ≥ 70% of days AND ≥ 30 units AND confidence not Low. A top-decile article that fails a floor is Solid and the row says which floor.
5. **Solid**: pD ≥ 0.50. 6. **Steady**: pD ≥ 0.20. 7. **Slow**: below.
Each row shows why (rank "7 of 284 (p97)", floors) and the nearest boundary ("Drops to Solid below p90 (1.2/day)"). Sell-through is no longer used to classify (it measures "was replenished", not demand).
**Actions** (`_siAxActionOf`, per row with its trigger): Reorder now = Stock-constrained, or Winner/Solid/Steady whose cover is shorter than the lead time (confidence not Low); Stock-out risk = cover within 2 weeks of the lead time, or a size out that sold in 28 days; Hold = cover > 26 weeks; Stop / clear = Dead; Stuck = Slow, 90+ counted days, cover > 12 weeks or unknown; Mark down = Slow with cover > 12 weeks; "Review: little data" = Slow with Low confidence.
**Lead times** are editable defaults, kept per device (`localStorage['groovy-si-leadtimes']`), never facts: tees and tops 21 days; hoodies, jackets, denim, bottoms 35; everything else 28. The screen labels them "default, unconfirmed" until edited; confidence stays capped at Medium until one is.
**Per-article lead time** (30 Sept 2026): any article can carry its own number, `localStorage['groovy-si-leadtimes-article']` (map CODE -> whole days, 1 to 365, corrupt or out-of-range entries ignored). ONE reader, `_siAxLeadTime`, resolves article override, then category default, then the global fallback (28 days); the action text says which applied ("your lead time for this article", "your lead time", or "default lead time, unconfirmed"). Set it with "Lead time: N days · Edit" on the article page and on every Overview row (Enter saves, Escape cancels, Reset to default removes it); a "custom" chip shows where an override applies and the lead-time panel lists them all with Reset. Saving recomputes the verdict, the action and the Overview tiles from data already in memory (no re-fetch). **Overrides are per device**, like the category defaults; a shared store (Firestore) would need a `firestore.rules` change and a republish, a later option. An article with its own lead time is not capped at Medium confidence for having a default one.
Constants `_SI_AX_SCORE`; none validated with the business, no backtest yet. A clause whose metric is "—" is skipped and the row says "partly unverified".
**Overview** (the tab opens on it): four tiles, Reorder now · Stock-out risk · Stuck / stop · Winners, each a count and a list of up to 10 rows (Show all), with Open and + Compare. No new Firestore reads.

## 3b. Needs Attention (1 Oct 2026; defaults, not facts)

One flat ranked list of ARTICLES (never variants), built on the Explorer's own helpers (`_siAxStats`, `_siAxClassify`, `_siAxActionOf`, `_siAxLeadTime`, `_siAxConfidence`, momentum, `sizeRows`) so the tab and the Explorer cannot disagree. All thresholds live in `_SI_NA`. Bands: **Critical** (act today), **Act this week**, **Watch** (collapsed; shown, not counted). Caps 5 / 8 / 5 with "Show all N"; every count (headline, tab pill, Overview tiles) is the UNCAPPED count from one computation (`_siNaState`). The pill and the "need action" figure are critical + act.

Evidence floor for any stock or demand claim: confidence above Low AND (10+ units in 28 days OR 1+ unit per in-stock day). One primary issue per article, in this order (others ride along as "also"): stock-out, run-out, size hole, rising on thin stock, demand drop, dead, overstock, data check.

| Issue | Rule | Band |
|---|---|---|
| Out of stock | 0 on hand, class Winner/Solid/Constrained, units28 > 0 (the Explorer calls zero recent sales "watch") | Critical |
| Runs out before restock | seller class, cover days < lead time (article override, category default, else 28) | Critical under half the lead time, else Act |
| Size hole | article cover >= lead; a size with >= 15% of 28-day sales is out (3+ units) or thin (4+ units, under 14 days of size cover); article 8+ units in 28 days | Act if out and (>= 20% share or >= 10 units), else Watch |
| Rising on thin stock | momentum Rising, cover between lead and lead + 28 days, not a sale (over 50% of last week's 10+ units below 85% of the usual price) | Act (a run-out carries a "rising" chip instead) |
| Demand drop | Winner/Solid, momentum Fading, in stock 70%+ of days; or last 7 days vs prior 4 weeks (base 5+, z < -2.5, in stock 5+ of 7 days) | Act if -40% or worse, else Watch |
| Dead | class Dead, 5+ on hand, 56+ counted days | Act at 100,000+ at selling price, else Watch |
| Overstocked | Slow with "stuck/markdown", or cover >= 26 weeks and 100,000+ at selling price | Act at 52+ weeks or 500,000+, else Watch |
| Data check | 10%+ of the article's units voided (5+) | Watch |

Winter stock (hoodie, zip, jacket, coat, fleece, sweat, or `season:winter`) between 15 Sept and 30 Nov is a **seasonal wait** (Watch, re-check 15 Nov), never a red clear-out. Value is on-hand x average selling price, labelled "at selling price" (cost is not stored). Missing price or pace is "—", never 0. Ranking inside a band: Rs at stake (lost sales for stock issues, value tied up for cash issues), then units, class, code.

Trust strip (above the list): red = stock history unreadable, stale snapshot (over 26 h, or its run failed), truncated snapshot, order sync failed or over 8 h; amber = returns not synced, missing snapshot days; a quiet "Data checks" line lists the cleaning counts and unknowns. Missing meta is "unknown", never fine. Red items tag every row "numbers may be off" and an empty list says "not an all-clear".

Situation view (`_siNaPlaybook(issue)`, pure): Situation, How to tackle (ordered, owners Raees / Mustafa / Daniyal / Saim, suggestions not assignments), Why it matters, What not to do, Key numbers (size table), How sure are we. Reorder guide = pace x (lead + 28 days, 35 for a Winner) - on hand, pace +/-25%, rounded up to packs of 12.

Where the planning notes disagreed: owners for ads (Daniyal, not Mustafa); a stale section falls back to Overview (not Attention); the situation replaces the list in place with Back/Esc/Prev/Next (not an inline panel) so scroll and band state restore like the SKU click-through; the pill counts critical + act (not Watch); stock-out requires recent sales (consistency with the Explorer over the plan's "out 45 days" case). Selling Patterns is removed (its charts duplicated the Explorer); `_siBarChart` stays for Weekly Close.

## 3c. Stock vs sales timeline (1 Oct 2026; one article; estimates, not facts)
Card "Stock vs sales" on the article page. Reads only what is already loaded (cleaned daily sales, `_siHist.byCode`, the lead time); **no new read**. Pure functions: `_siAxTimeline` (series, stretches, restocks), `_siAxTlWindow`/`_siAxTlSummary` (the visible slice), `_siAxTlReadout` (the plain sentences), `_siAxTlCoverage` (the honest caption), `_siAxTlColumns` (day or Monday-start week).
- **Day state** uses the Explorer's own stock-out rule: out when stock read 0 at BOTH the previous and that day's close; in when either was above 0; either close missing = **no data** (a gap, never a zero). The day stock first reads 0 is still an in-stock day.
- **Stretch** = consecutive out days; it continues across up to 2 no-data days (one missing snapshot) when stock read 0 on both sides, and those days are not counted as out days. A stretch starting at the first reading or at a gap is flagged "or earlier".
- **Lost-sales estimate** per stretch = days out x in-stock pace. Pace = mean sold per in-stock day over up to 28 in-stock days before it and up to 28 after it (the first 3 days after the restock are skipped, a launch burst); each side needs 14+ in-stock days and 5+ units. Both sides: range [lower, higher]; one side only: [0.5 x pace, pace] (an assumption, said on screen); neither: not estimated, with the reason. A stretch over 42 days is not estimated (probably discontinued). Rupees = units x the article's average price before discounts. It ignores customers who bought another size or colour, so it is an upper-ish bound for the article.
- **Restocks** are inferred per day: stock minus the previous close plus that day's sales is at least max(5, 10% of the previous stock) (the received rule). Solid triangle = 20+ units and 25%+ of the stock before; hollow = small. Never called "received".
- **Weeks**: stock = last close of the week, out when 4+ of its days (or all of a shorter first/last week) were out; sales are summed. Range chips 30 days / 90 days / All (sales from the first synced order, stock from the first snapshot; days before it are hatched "no stock data").
- **Lead-time marker** (`_siAxLeadTime`, article override included): an order placed on (today - lead time) lands today; each stretch's "Order by" is its start minus the lead time.
- **Coverage caption** states the snapshot span, gaps left blank, the negative-stock clamp (oversold variants are counted per day only), early snapshots re-dated to the day they close, and the location boundary: snapshots carry `locations_seen` only once stock is summed over every location, so `_siAxBuildHistory` keeps `loc` = {max, missing, firstSeen}; when max > 1 and earlier snapshots lack it, the caption says stock before `firstSeen` may be understated.
- Not built here: cover line, compare availability strip, per-size stock timeline (plans 03 P1/P2).

## 3d. Sizes card (1 Oct 2026, cache v283; one article; defaults, not facts)
Below Stock vs sales on the article page. Rows are `_siAxStats(a).sizeRows` (no new read), states come from `_siNaSizeRows`, so the card and Needs Attention agree. Sorted garment sizes, then waist, then other, Unknown last.
- **Sales curve** = per size `w × (28-day share) + (1 − w) × (lifetime share)`, `w = recent ÷ (recent + 6)`, renormalised to 100% (a size with few recent units leans on its lifetime share; no recent sales anywhere gives the lifetime curve). **Stock curve** = on hand ÷ the article's on hand. Drawn as two aligned HTML bars per size; the table always sits under them.
- **Cover per size** = on hand ÷ (28-day units ÷ 4); "—" with no recent sales or no stock data. Per-size stock history is not loaded (history is per article), so there is no per-size in-stock pace: cover is an at-most figure for a size that was out part of the window.
- **Chips** (glyph + word): Out and selling (0 on hand, sold in 28 d) · Thin (under 14 days of cover with 4+ units in 28 d — the Needs Attention rule, not the 7 days first proposed, so the tab and card agree) · Over-stocked vs demand (more than 26 weeks of cover, or 2× its sales share in stock with 8+ units and over 12 weeks of cover) · No sales (none in 28 d) · No stock data · Fine.
- **Readout**: sizes whose sales share beats their stock share by 10+ points ("S and M carry 58% of sales but only 21% of stock") and the reverse ("XL is 31% of stock and 6% of sales").
- **Suggested split of the next batch**: the sales curve over real sizes (Unknown excluded), at least 3% for a size that sold 3+ units, nothing for a size that sold none, refused under 30 counted units or fewer than two sizes that sold. Percent and units sum exactly (largest remainder). Stock is not netted off. The batch total is the Needs Attention reorder guide (`_siNaQty`, high end split) or "set the total in your PO"; no second calculator. Labelled a suggestion, not an order; a "do not reorder" verdict is repeated beside it.
- **Colour is not split.** Dump check (30 Sept, 387 SKU prefixes): 26 carry more than one colour value, and those are catch-all prefixes (OVERS, CARGO, TOPS, FOG, VII, Y2K) or label noise (GB001 "acid washed / mineral wash", GST003 "mint green / blank"); line items carry no per-variant colour on a real article. An article here is one colour.
- Limits: sales are censored at zero stock (an out size reads low), returns are not synced (sales run high), K=6, 3%, 30 units, 10 points and 12 weeks are defaults.

## 3e. Portfolio (1 Oct 2026, cache v284; the whole range; defaults, not facts)
A sub-view beside Overview, Search and Compare (`_siAxPortfolioBody`, pure core `_siAxPfCalc`, readout `_siAxPfReadout`). Built only on the Explorer's own indexes (`_siAxStats`, `_siAxClassify`, `_siAxDemand`, `_siAxTimeline`), so its numbers agree with Overview, Needs Attention and the article pages; cached per data load; no Firestore read. Articles = those with counted sales or stock (the Overview's set). Missing is "—", never 0.
- **Class mix**: articles, % of articles, % of units, % of revenue per class, as three stacked bars and a table. Revenue is unit price x quantity before discounts and before later returns.
- **Category** (the Explorer's category, "Unknown" when blank) and **Season**: sales are split by the month of each order (Oct-Jan winter, else other: Feb-Sep). An article is a winter seller only when MORE than half its units sold in winter months (exactly half is not). Stock and cover follow the article. Stock value = on hand x the article's average selling price (before discounts); an article with stock and no price is counted and flagged, not valued. Group cover = group stock / group weekly pace (the headline pace), over articles that have both.
- **Concentration (Pareto)**: smallest number of articles whose cumulative share reaches 50% / 80% of units and of revenue (exactly 50% counts), top 5/10/20 shares, ranked list with cumulative share (15, then Show all). The readout calls the top 20 holding 50%+ of units concentrated and under 35% spread out (defaults).
- **Cash tied up**: each article in ONE bucket, in order: dead, slow, winner, over-stocked (Solid/Steady/Stock-constrained with 26+ weeks of cover), everything else; value at selling price. "Stuck" = dead + slow + over-stocked: under 20% of stock value is a light share, over 40% heavy (defaults).
- **Stock-out exposure** (needs the stock history): the sum, over the last 90 days, of the Stock vs sales card's own estimate (`_siAxTlSummary`), so an article gets an estimate only where those rules allow one; stretches without one are counted, not guessed. Shown as a range of units and revenue at list price, labelled an estimate. Selling articles out of stock now are counted separately.
- **Every article**: ranked table (always present) with filters by class, category and season and four orders (units per in-stock day, longest cover, stock value, units); a scatter of weeks of cover (52+ on the right edge) against units per in-stock day with HTML dots (hidden on phones; the table has the same articles). Clicking a name or dot opens the article (the Overview's Open). Missing values sort last.
- The trust banner and a Portfolio caveat (returns not synced, discounts, selling price not cost) are carried over.
- Not built (plan 09 P2): category treemap, cannibalisation clusters, margin, weekly catalogue trend.

## Loading screen, honest percentage and retry (1 Oct 2026, cache v287)

Inventory Intel's first open reads about 55k documents. The loader (`js/shopify.js`, "Loading, honest percentage and retry") replaces the shimmer-only page.

- **Percentage is stage-based and real.** Seven stages, weights 4 catalog, 10 orders, 50 line items, 1 weekly closes, 14 stock snapshot, 3 sync status, 18 counting the stock (the first index build) = 100. A stage counts only when its read returned. No timer, no creep, monotonic, capped at 99 until `_siLoaded` is true. The weights are a hypothesis from the dump sizes; retune from real timings. The 150-snapshot history is not in the percentage (the page is usable without it).
- **Display rules.** Overlay only if the load is still running at 300ms, kept at least 500ms once shown, then a 400ms settle. Nothing on a second open (data held). After 20s the stage is named as slow; at 90s the stage is marked failed and named. Counts ("2,126 catalog entries", "N line items") appear only once their read has returned. It sits inside the page (a host in the skeleton), never `position:fixed`, so the sidebar and Back stay clickable.
- **Retry re-reads only what failed.** Every read is its own promise and assigns its own result, so one refusal no longer discards the rest. Auto-retry twice with jittered backoff (about 2s, then 5s), a visible "Line items failed — retrying in Ns… (attempt k of 3)". Permission-denied and timeouts are never auto-retried; a 429 waits at least 10s and gets one automatic retry; cap 3 attempts per stage per visit. The final card names the stage, the last error and the attempt count, with Retry this stage, Retry everything (only when 2+ failed) and Copy details. One controller with a generation counter; a busy stage ignores a second press; leaving the page cancels every timer.
- **Unchanged on purpose:** a critical failure shows the error card and never a partial page; meta/history failures stay silent; the stock-history read keeps its own existing retry (no countdown yet).
- Not seen on a real screen or phone; weights and timings are not measured against live Firestore.

## Needs Attention: reasons, bands and clickable Overview tiles (1 Oct 2026, cache v288)

Interface and one new list; the detectors and the Overview tile counts are unchanged.

- **Band chips** ("32 critical", "69 this week", "89 to watch") are buttons (`aria-pressed`). Pressing one isolates that band (every row, nothing folded), scrolls to it and flashes it once (static outline under reduced motion); pressing it again returns to all. A band and a reason combine.
- **Reason chips** replace Stock / Cash tied up / Demand / Data, in severity order, each with a live count that IS its list's length (`_siNaReasonList`, one source): **Urgent restocks** (out of stock, runs out before restock, size hole), **Sales loss**, **High return rate**, **Overstocked**, **Dead stock**, **Demand shifts** (rising on thin stock, demand drop). An article may sit under several. "Cash tied up" was exactly Overstocked + Dead stock, so it is no chip (the Overview tile still reaches that union as `cash`). **"Data" is no chip**; the voided-units detection, the trust strip and "Data checks" are untouched. Pressing the active chip returns to All. A reason opens the watch band (a list the owner asked for is not folded). Every row opens the situation view of THAT reason.
- **Sales loss** = articles with an estimated 5+ units lost to stock-outs in the last 90 days, the Portfolio's own per-article estimate (`_siAxPfLostOf`, timeline rules; range, in-stock pace only), shown as an estimate with rupees at the average price. Band: the stock-out/run-out band if the article has one, else watch. Unavailable (no stock history) shows "—", never 0.
- **High return rate** (defaults): per article over the last 90 counted days, rate = refunded units / (net units + refunded units); needs 20+ such units, a rate of 15%+ AND at least 2x the median rate of the articles that pass the 20-unit floor, and a gap bigger than chance (one-sided z >= 1.645 against the median floored at 2%, with continuity correction). Act at 25%+, else watch. **Only when returns are synced** (95% of the last 60 days' line items carry `status_synced_at`). Verified on the real dump of 30 Sept 2026: 51,176 line items, none with `status_synced_at` or `refunded_quantity` (13 `partially_refunded`, 1 `refunded` by financial status), so returns are NOT synced there and the chip reads "Return rate: waiting for returns sync" with "—"; clicking it explains how it lights up. The quiet returns-gap note is kept while unsynced and goes when synced.
- **Overview tiles** (Reorder now, Stock-out risk, Stuck / stop, Winners) say what they mean and what to do under the count, and each row has a **Situation** button that opens the Needs Attention situation view in place (Back names the tile, Prev/Next walk the tile's list, Esc closes). The tile's action maps to the closest playbook type (reorder: stock-out or run-out; risk: size hole; stuck: overstock or dead; winners: a new "top seller" playbook); the article's own detected issue is used when there is one, otherwise the same issue object is built from the same helpers. Counts and the lead-time control ("Lead time: N days · Edit", per-article override, category defaults) are unchanged.
- New playbooks (owners are suggestions): sales loss (Raees reorder point and reorder now if out, Mustafa back-in-stock, Daniyal ad timing), high return rate (Mustafa return reasons and size chart, Saim fit and pattern, Raees hold reorder and QC, Daniyal pause push), top seller (Raees cover, Daniyal, Mustafa no discount, Saim sibling colourway). Tests: `tests/shopify-na-reasons.test.js`.

## Stock history: who needs which days, and what changed (1 Oct 2026, cache v289, load-time stage 1)

The history is `shopify_inventory_snapshots`, newest 150 daily documents (128 exist, 26 May to 30 Sept 2026; about 283 KB each, ~36 MB, measured from the dump). **Every figure below reads days back to the start of the article's counted window (`_siAxStats`: live date or first synced order, up to today), so each needs the WHOLE history, not a recent slice.**

| Consumer | Window it reads |
|---|---|
| `_siAxStats` via `_siAxExposure`: in-stock rate, in-stock/out days, measured days, units per in-stock day, headline pace basis, sell-through run | counted window to today (whole history) |
| `_siAxPaceEwma` (recency-weighted pace), cover | last 12 weeks |
| class (percentile of units per in-stock day), Overview scorecard counts, Needs Attention state, the tab pill, Overview tiles, Portfolio exposure and lost sales | follow `_siAxStats` (whole history) |
| `_siAxTimeline` / the Stock vs sales timeline, "All" range | from first synced order to today (whole history); 28/90 day ranges are the last N days of it |
| `_siNaSudden` (sudden stop) | last 7 days |
| Data trust: missing days, incomplete snapshot | last N days; "incomplete" is judged against the median item count of ALL days read |
| Explorer note (snapshot count and range), the text export | whole history |

**Why there is no lower cap and no first-60-then-the-rest read.** Measured on the real dump (cap of newest 60 vs all 128): 278 of 386 articles changed in-stock figures and 53 changed class. Every first-screen figure (Overview tiles, pill, classes) needs the whole window, so a partial read could only show pending anyway; it would add a second read and save nothing. `_SI_HIST_MAX` stays 150.

**What changed (no figure changes).**
- Each document is folded once (`_siAxFoldDoc`: date, snapshot time, item count, locations, per-code stock sums, oversold counts) and the history is built from folds (`_siAxHistoryFromFolds`). `_siAxBuildHistory` is the same function as before; a verbatim copy of the old code is the reference in `tests/shopify-history-cost.test.js` (identical on the real dump: 874,894 characters of history, byte for byte).
- Folds of FINAL days (up to two days ago) are cached in `localStorage['groovy-si-histfold']` (about 0.65 MB for 128 days). A warm visit reads only `date >` the newest cached day (2 documents: yesterday and today, about 0.6 MB) and rebuilds the same history. A cold visit, Retry, a cache older than 7 days since its last full read, another format version or unreadable storage is the old full read.
- With a warm cache the read starts WITH the page load (it is tiny), so it has usually landed by first paint. Cold, it still starts after the first paint, so it does not compete with the line items for bandwidth. The percentage and stage names are unchanged (history stays outside it).
- While it loads, the Overview shows "Stock history: loading…" above the two tiles, which show "…" (never 0); the pill is absent; Explorer figures show "—". A failed read shows the reason with Retry (a full read); the cache is not touched.
- Known limits: a snapshot edited retroactively after it was cached is seen only after the 7-day expiry or a Retry; a document that fails to fold still takes a slot in the cold read's 150 but not in a warm one (none exist today).
- Not measured here: network time. Reads and bytes (per visit, history only): cold 128 documents (~36 MB), warm 2 (~0.6 MB; estimated from the average document size).

## Ignore: one shared list (1 Oct 2026, cache v290, product-data sub-phase 1 of 3)

Anyone who can open Inventory Intel can ignore an article; everyone sees the same lists. Collection `inventory_article_meta/{CODE}` (doc id = the upper-case article code). Fields written now: `code, ignoredUntil (YYYY-MM-DD or null), ignoreForever, ignoredAt, ignoredBy (username), updatedAt, updatedBy`; `type` (top/bottom/other) and `season` (winter/summer/all) are validated by the rules but nothing writes them yet (sub-phase 2).
- **The one question:** "Remind me in…" 1 week / 2 weeks / 1 month / 3 months / 6 months (1 month preselected) plus a tick "Never remind me". No reason field. Dates are PKT: `ignoredUntil` is the LAST ignored day (today + 7 / 14 days, or + N calendar months clamped to the month end, e.g. 31 Jan + 1 month = 28 Feb); the article is hidden through 23:59:59.999 PKT of that day and is back the day after. Nothing is written at expiry; the client compares at read time (`_siIgnored`, client clock), and the lists recompute at each PKT midnight (the memo key carries the day). An open tab left past midnight updates on its next repaint or navigation.
- **Excluded from** (all through `_siAxLive()`, the full index minus ignored): Needs Attention list, chips, counts and the tab pill; the Overview tiles; Explorer Overview rows, tiles and category list; the Portfolio rollups; the class counts; the Explorer's header count (shows "N articles (K ignored)").
- **Still using the FULL list, on purpose** (four lines in `js/shopify.js`, held by a test): Search (an ignored article is found and marked "Ignored until D" / "Ignored for good" so it can be restored), Compare, the Ignored tab, and the demand pool that percentiles classes (so ignoring one article never changes another article's class).
- **Not filtered (SKU-level ledgers, not article lists):** the four Overview stat cards, Daily Movement, SKU Table, Weekly Close, Advanced. Their figures are stock facts; hiding an ignored article there would make Inventory Value wrong.
- **Ignore buttons:** article page header, each Needs Attention row and its situation view, each Explorer Overview row. The **Ignored** tab (before Advanced, with a count) lists each article with picture, title, code, class, the date it returns (or "never"), who ignored it and Restore.
- **The read:** ONE `getDocs` per page load, started with the loader, outside the percentage; it never rejects. A failed read shows a quiet "Ignore list could not be read" with Retry on Overview, Needs Attention and the Explorer and hides nothing.
- **Writes:** merge write of the allowed fields, optimistic (memory first; a refusal restores the previous state with a toast; offline says "Saved on this device — will sync"), silent-save opt-out, audit verbs `Article ignored` / `Article restored` (Monitor bucket: Edit). Restore clears the four ignore fields (no delete; the rules refuse delete).
- **Rules need a republish** (own commit). Lead-time logic is unchanged. Not seen on a real screen.

## Type & season feed (6 Oct 2026, cache v300, product-data sub-phase 3 of 3)

The saved `type` (top/bottom/other) and `season` (winter/summer/all) from the Type & season queue now feed three places, all through ONE reader, `_siMetaOf(code)`. No `firestore.rules` change; lead-time logic untouched.

- **Explorer filter (Overview, Search, Portfolio; not Compare).** A Season row (Winter / Summer / All-season / Unclassified) and a Type row (Top / Bottom / Other / Unclassified), each with an "Any" chip. Each chip count is taken under the OTHER filter (faceted), over articles with sales or stock, so the buckets add up to Any. "Unclassified" = no saved value, its own bucket. A line says "Showing N of M … K hidden by these filters" with Clear. Search says how many more matches are hidden; Compare ignores the filter and hides the bar (articles are picked by name). Needs Attention and its counts do NOT read the filter (`_siNaState` stays the single source). Default is no filter.
- **Portfolio.** Two new rollups, "By saved type" and "By saved season", built by the Category grouping function inside `_siAxPfCalc` (no second calculator): articles, units, revenue at list price, sell-through = units sold ÷ (units sold + units on hand) over articles with stock data, in-stock days (mean of each article's in-stock share of measured days), Dead and Slow counts (class), stock at selling price, and cash tied up = stock value in the dead, slow and over-stocked buckets (the cash table's own `bucketOf`). Each article is in exactly one row per table. The older order-month table is now titled "By selling window (order month)" (and its list filter "Selling window") so the two seasons cannot be confused: selling window = when it sold, saved season = what staff say it is.
- **Needs Attention seasonal wait.** The fixed 15 Sept to 30 Nov window (`_SI_NA.seasonFrom/seasonTo`, `_siNaWinter`) is kept only as the fallback. With a SAVED season the article is judged inside its window and waits outside it: **season map, owner decision 6 Oct 2026:** winter Oct, Nov, Dec, Jan (the Explorer's `_SI_AX_WINTER_MONTHS`), summer Feb to Sep, all-season always judged; no grace period (winter articles are judged from 1 Oct), September is summer so there is no gap. A wait stays a Watch (never a red clear-out), flagged seasonal. **This tightens one thing:** a saved winter article that is dead on 1 Oct to 30 Nov is now judged (before, a hoodie-like article was excused until 30 Nov). Summer articles wait Oct to Jan; winter articles wait Feb to Sep. No saved season: today's rule (category/tags, 15 Sept to 30 Nov), the playbook says "Season unknown", and where that guess decides the band (the legacy window is open) the confidence drops one step. List unreadable: same fallback, "Type and season unavailable".
- **Unreadable or still-loading saved list:** no filter is applied (every article shown), the bar says "Type/season unavailable" with Retry (or "Reading the saved types and seasons…"), the rollups say unavailable, never zeros.
- Defaults, not facts: the Feb to Sep and Oct to Jan windows are the owner's season map and the Explorer's order-month rule; every month is in exactly one of them.
- Tests: `tests/shopify-typeseason-feed.test.js` (hand-computed 8-article fixture, every rule reverted once and caught). Not seen on a real screen or against real saved data.

## Already on order: open POs in Needs Attention (6 Oct 2026, cache v302; ASSUMPTIONS, not facts)
Inventory Intel never read `pos`, so an article already being cut still read "Reorder now". On the 30 Sept dump (read from the files, today pinned to 1 Oct, no live calls): 71 Reorder-now rows, **12 have open pieces on order**; in Needs Attention 4 issues would drop one band (GP001, GB001, GH022 to Act this week, GH013 to Watch), 3 get a smaller guide (GST001, GST069, GST068), counts go critical 32 to 29, act 69 to 71, watch 89 to 90, action 101 to 100, total 190 unchanged. 92 of 152 POs read as arrived by inference, 44 open, 16 expired.
- **The read:** `pos`, once per page load, `where('ts','>=', now - 120 days)` with `limit(400)` (single-field range, no index). The 30 Sept dump holds 152 POs, about 460 KB (the whole `pos` read is what `loadData` already does for the PO pages). Never rejects; a failed read is state `error` and every article answers "unavailable", never an empty list. Started with the page load, outside the percentage stages. Any signed-in user and the QA account can read `pos` (rule `isQaRead() || signedIn()`); the PO write rule is untouched and there is no new collection.
- **What a PO gives:** `code` (cleaned like a SKU), `qty`, `createdAt` (else the PKT day of `ts`). A row with no code, no day or qty 0 is dropped. PO `poStatus` and stage completion are ignored on purpose (all staged POs sit at `cutting`, none has a `doneAt`).
- **Open until arrival or expiry (`_SI_PO`, `_siPoCalc`, pure):** oldest PO first. A PO has ARRIVED when a day after its creation day shows an unexplained stock rise (stock change plus units sold, the Explorer's own residual) of at least **max(5, 50% of the PO qty)**; one jump explains several POs (its size is used up as it is matched). Otherwise it EXPIRES **45 days after it was raised** (age 45 or more). Both numbers are assumptions for the owner. With no stock history for the article only the expiry applies and the chip title says so.
- **Used by Needs Attention (`_siNaOnOrder`, the only place):** for an issue with a reorder guide (stock-out, run-out, rising) the guide is reduced by the on-order pieces, each end rounded up to a pack of 12, never below 0. Covered means the top of the guide reaches 0: the band drops ONE step (Critical to Act this week, Act to Watch), the guide is gone, the situation text says why and until about when (the earliest expiry among the open POs). Partly covered: the guide shrinks and the band stays. Nothing is hidden: the row stays, with a chip "On order: N pcs (PO date)" or "N POs, latest date". Counts, pill, headline and Overview tiles all come from `_siNaState`, whose memo is keyed on the PO read, so they agree. The Overview Reorder-now row, its situation view (same detected issue) and the Explorer verdict show the chip; the Overview reorder TILE count is the action-key count and is not changed.
- **Unavailable:** read failed means no downgrade and no reduction, a note "On-order data unavailable" with Retry on Needs Attention and the Reorder tile, a line in the situation's confidence text. Still loading: no change, no note.
- **Unverified:** the 45 days and the 50% rise (92 inferred arrivals on the dump: median 10 days after the PO, p75 24 days, 6 within 3 days, so a stray restock or return can read as a PO arriving too early, which UNDER-counts what is on order); that a PO's `code` is the Shopify article code for every PO (non-Shopify codes such as AP023 simply never match); that Raees raises a PO for the order that the batch actually covers (a PO qty is read as the pieces to arrive, not per size); a real signed-in session and live PO data (not run here).
Tests: `tests/shopify-on-order.test.js` (hand-computed, 21 breaks each caught by name); layout fragment `inventory intel — on order chip, rows, situation and unavailable note`.
