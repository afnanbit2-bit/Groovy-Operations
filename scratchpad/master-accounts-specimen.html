Every figure is a sample, not the books. Switch page, width and theme here; on a page, click a concern, a row or a stat to open its rail.

What each page is for — one question each (plan §16.1)

Todayma-overview

Where is the money right now, and what needs me? Cash in hand by holder, three concerns, this week's due-out against expected-in; the 30 days, the spend list and aging below.

Moneyma-money

What does each holder hold, and is it reconciled? Balances, last counts, pending confirmations; a holder's statement, transfers, the MCB import.

Money inma-in

What is owed to us, and what came in? Couriers (CPRs to collect, TCS, Blue-Ex, Bykea), Online (Payfast payouts), Warehouse & gate, Customers.

Money outma-out

What do we owe, what is due, what did we pay? Pay days, bills, purchase orders, the commitments register (rent, utilities, subscriptions), payroll, petty and floats.

Partiesma-parties

Who do we deal with, and where do we stand with each? One list; a party's terms, rate card, ledger, statement and aging in the rail.

Ledgerma-ledger

Every posting, one shape, with every label it carries; a document and its edit history in the rail; the Unlabelled queue.

Costingma-costing

What does a piece cost, and what do we make on it? A cost sheet per production PO with a missing leg named, never zero.

Savingsma-savings

The owners' book: accounts, the loan to Groovy, targets with an ETA, subscriptions per owner, net worth.

Reportsma-reports

The statements: P&L against last period and budget, balance sheet, cash flow, aging, "of every ₨100", tax, budgets, insights.

Close & auditma-close

Is the period done, and who did what? The quarter checklist, the month soft close, backups, the audit trail, Download the books, settings.

The rules this specimen follows (plan §16.4)

  • One spacing scale (4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32), a 1100px content column, no card inside a card; sections sit on the surface and only the rail, a form or an error is boxed.
  • Type at the app's sizes: 22/600 page title, 15/600 section title, 14px body and tables, a 28px hero. No uppercase, no letter-spacing, nothing under 11px.
  • At most four figures in a row, unbordered; every other number is a table row. Tables are 40px rows with the money right-aligned in tabular figures.
  • A concern is a dot and a sentence with its action as a link; fine is silent; at most one banner on a page.
  • One chart per section, 240px, with "Show as table" beneath; series colours only inside a plot; semantic colours as dots and words, never fills.

Where the detail lives

  • The page shows the list; the rail (380px, sticky) shows the thing you clicked — a concern's basis and six-month history, a party's terms and rate card, a document and its edit history. Under 1200px it slides over; on the phone it is a bottom sheet.
  • Whatever explains a number lives in the rail or a tooltip, never on the page.
  • The Record button on every page opens the same document picker; the page only prefills.

Where the rules came from (plan §16.5)

  • A measured audit of the v3 specimen in headless Chromium: the overview was 2,532px tall, 82% of its text at 12px or under, 66 bordered boxes, the first chart at y = 1,183.
  • The Polaris, Carbon and Primer design systems, read from their repositories: table cell padding 6, 32–40px rows for finance density, tabular numbers, units in the header, wrap not truncate, one banner or none, one primary action, skeletons over spinners.
  • Both are model output, kept in the scratchpad beside this file; the plan says which number won where they disagreed.
